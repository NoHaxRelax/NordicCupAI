How well does the guide deliver?

1,000 random maps · native energy and movement · 60-second limit · one encounter per map

Delivery means the predator stayed within 40 units of bait and sensed it for 10 seconds. It does not establish full-game retention. Bait stays at full energy; the guide uses normal energy.

What this test measures

All static map edges are known. The guide starts with full energy and sees a predator 80–160 units away. Only ordinary observations guide its decisions. A bait site must have a second geometric access point. Missing sites and computational timeouts remain in the overall denominator.

“Detected” is the share of recorded ticks while the guide was alive where the tracked predator could see or hear it. “Guide alive” means alive when the run ended, which may be earlier than 60 seconds.
