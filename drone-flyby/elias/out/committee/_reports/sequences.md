Fit H on the EMPTY renders (ORB + MAGSAC, 6200 to 6900 inliers of 7500 to 8300 matches), scale it so sH - I has rank one (residual 1e-5 to 1.6e-4), set A = (sH-I)/2. Validated against the exact odd-pose annotations: median full-step error 0.74 px, median HALF-STEP error 0.57 px over all 12 steps. Evidence: elias/out/committee/sequences/homography_probe.py.
