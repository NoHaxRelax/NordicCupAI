# backgrounds

## measured facts
- RATE. F3 (elias/release/both_m1280.pt, the deployed checkpoint) invents 1.286 BIRTH-GRADE false positives per delivered L1 view on ground that holds nothing, and 0.091 per L2 view. Birth-grade = confidence >= 0.25 AND the box strictly inside the delivered view, which is exactly the tracker's birth rule (tracking/revisit.py line 384 with line 290). Measured on Oscar's 468 object-free 4K renders over 14 new sites, cut exactly as our sweep delivers them (L1: 1920x1080 top-band regions at centres 960/1920/2880, y=540, INTER_AREA to 960x540; L2: four native 960x540 tiles at y=270). Evidence: elias/out/committee/backgrounds/tables-empty.md, run `empty`, 1404 L1 views and 1872 L2 views, script bg_fp.py, 350 s on the laptop GPU. Raw thresholds F3 L1: 5.369 at conf 0.05, 2.268 at 0.15, 1.323 at 0.25, 0.419 at 0.5.
- F5 IS 4.5x CLEANER. F5_fixed_m1280.pt gives 0.289 birth-grade per L1 view and 0.019 per L2 view against F3's 1.286 and 0.091. Same file, same views, same call. This is the opposite ranking to the validation flight, whose backgrounds were in F3's training set.
- ONE CLASS CARRIES IT. F3 at L1, birth-grade per view: small_launcher 1.135 (88 % of the total), spacecraft 0.036, ta-ta 0.026, tank 0.024, medium_launcher 0.019, medium_plane 0.017, small_tower 0.010, jet_plane 0.009, everything else under 0.005; hangar, large_launcher, large_tower and condor never birthed once. F5 at L1: small_launcher 0.192, medium_launcher 0.032, ta-ta 0.028, spacecraft 0.010, rest under 0.01. Over L1+L2, 1708 of F3's 1976 birth-grade false boxes (86 %) are small_launcher.
- TERRAIN. Birth-grade per view by my site typing (place names; the measured terrain composition of each site's views is in tables-empty.md): fields F3 4.78 / F5 0.77, airfield 0.94 / 0.24, coast 0.67 / 0.35, port 0.50 / 0.10, urban 0.49 / 0.17, forest 0.36 / 0.16. One site dominates: nl_flevoland, a ploughed polder, 8.96 per view for F3 (lane0 alone 18.85 over 39 L1 views). Dropping that site entirely, F3 L1 is still 0.588 per view against F5's 0.198.
- WHAT IT FIRES ON. Contact sheet elias/out/committee/backgrounds/fp_F3_flevoland.jpg: F3 answers small_launcher at confidence 0.72 to 0.84 on bare furrow texture with nothing on it. That is ABOVE the median confidence of a true small_launcher detection (0.47), which is what makes those boxes expensive under a rank-based AP. fp_F3_all.jpg shows the two other modes: spacecraft at 0.80 to 0.88 on cross-shaped roof structures and small_tower at 0.84 to 0.87 on square flat roofs.
- THE ABSOLUTE NUMBER DOES NOT TRANSFER (my main caveat). Same geometry and code on REAL pixels: F3 L1 birth-grade is 0.080 per view on the organisers' 25 Helsinki reference frames (runs/ctrl_helsinki) and 0.124 on the validation flight counting only boxes that match no team label (runs/ctrl_validation, every third frame; that is an upper bound because the team labels are known to miss objects). Two of Oscar's sites (fi_helsinki_central, fi_malmi) are Helsinki orthophotos through the same render pipeline and read 0.41 and 0.91. So at least a factor of 5 to 11 of the jump from 0.08 to 1.29 is the render domain, not terrain novelty. Everything measured on BOTH sides of the same pipeline still transfers: F3 against F5, which class, which terrain, which confidence.
- A BIRTH COSTS 21.4 ANSWER BOXES. elias/out/harness/W_combo_ref/local.jsonl has 43 `birth` plus 18 `entry_birth` events and 1303 unique answer boxes over 249 frames. At the F3 rate that is about 320 false tracks and 6800 false boxes in a 249-frame run. The answer is capped at 500 boxes and 100 per class (tracking/revisit.py line 506); at the average rate the cap is not reached, but at the flevoland rate the steady state is roughly 190 live false small_launcher tracks (8.96 x 21.4 - a calculation from the two measurements, not a live run) and the per-class cap would start discarding real small_launchers.
- WHY ELEVEN CLASSES ARE SAFE AND TWO ARE NOT. Confidence of TRUE detections (gt_iou >= 0.5) on the validation flight's L1 top-band views, F3, p10 / median: large_launcher 0.92/0.94, hangar 0.89/0.91, small_tower 0.87/0.88, large_tower 0.87/0.90, jet_plane 0.85/0.89, mine_roller 0.84/0.92, helicopter 0.82/0.85, medium_plane 0.71/0.85, tank 0.70/0.84, medium_launcher 0.12/0.52, small_launcher 0.18/0.47. The birth-grade false boxes have median confidence 0.41 (max 0.91, median size 13x13 delivered px at L1). AP is rank-based, so the eleven classes whose true detections sit at 0.70 to 0.94 are barely touched; the two that overlap the false distribution are exactly the two that hallucinate.
- AP ESTIMATE. ap_cost.py (model stated in its header: recall assumed unchanged, each false birth worth 21.4 boxes, 249-frame run, so the number is the loss caused by false boxes alone) gives a macro multiplier over the 11 measurable classes of 0.818 for F3 and 0.905 for F5. Per class for F3: small_launcher 0.018, medium_launcher 0.213, medium_plane 0.900, tank 0.907, small_tower 0.962, the other six 1.000. Applied to F3's known portal per-class table the total would fall from about 0.754 to about 0.683, i.e. about 0.07 of the final score IF the unseen terrain hallucinates like these renders. Discounted by the render-domain factor above, the realistic exposure is 0.01 to 0.03.
- THE EXISTING TERRAIN PRIOR IS THE WRONG TOOL. Surroundings of F3's 1976 birth-grade false boxes by the runtime colour rules (elias/data/terrain.py classify): paved 60 %, water 12 %, forest 10 %, other 12 %, grass 3 %, sand 3 %. ELIAS_CONTEXT only down-weights water and forest, so it reaches 22 % of them and leaves the flevoland field boxes untouched. Checklist row B5 read it neutral on the harness; this extends that to fourteen new sites. (I also checked whether the pale patches in the contact sheet are a magenta render artefact: 0 of 400 sampled false boxes sit on a majority-magenta surface, the median hue is 30 to 60 deg, bare soil. So they are soil, not an artefact.)
- A PER-CLASS BIRTH FLOOR PROTECTS ELEVEN CLASSES BUT CANNOT TOUCH THE TWELFTH. protect.py, F3, L1: setting each class's floor just under the lowest true confidence ever observed for it removes 133 of 1805 false births (7 %) for 14 of 256 true birth-capable detections (5 %), and all of that 5 % is in small_launcher and medium_launcher, whose true detections run down to 0.05 and 0.08.
- THE ENSEMBLE MERGE AND ROUTING, REPLAYED EXACTLY. merge_sim.py replays elias/ensemble.py's mean-of-two pooling on detections already measured for both models on identical views. small_launcher at L1: F3 alone 1593 false births and 34 true; F3+F5 merged (class left unrouted) 729 false and 29 true; F5 alone (class routed) 270 false and 29 true. Over all classes the merge moves 1.390 to 0.664 false births per view (denominator 1299 views that produced any detection) while keeping 228 of 242 true births.
- AN ASPECT LEAD, THIN. The false small_launcher boxes are square (median w/h 1.00, p10-p90 0.89 to 1.08); the true ones are taller than wide (median 0.85, p10-p90 0.70 to 0.95). A gate at w/h <= 0.90 on F3 keeps 171 of 1593 false births (89 % removed) and 28 of 34 true ones. Thirty-four true detections from one flight is a thin basis - I mark this as a lead, not a result.

- expected gain: On the validation flight 0.00 to -0.005, inside portal noise. On the unseen flight +0.01 to +0.03. The range is the render-domain discount: the direction and the 5.9x ratio are measured on both sides of the same pipeline and transfer, the absolute false-positive rate is inflated by a factor of 5 to 11 by Oscar's render pipeline (ctrl_helsinki control). It also removes the per-class-cap and tracker-load hazard at field-type terrain.

The same-day rule in research/03-checklist.md says to use the 0.34 figure. Even the pessimistic bound is one class, 1/13 of the macro.

Nothing in elias/data/synth_yolo.py has to change - extra images plus empty .txt files go into the existing train split. These backgrounds are safe training data: Oscar's empty renders are new sites and contain no validation pixels (datasets/README.md, 'Leakage control').

- expected gain: +0.01 to +0.04 on an unseen flight if the fine-tune leaves the rest of F3 intact; 0 to -0.05 if it does not. Wide because every retrain so far (F5, F6, F7, F8) lost to F3 on something. A 3-epoch fine-tune FROM both_m1280.pt at lr0 0.002 is a different proposition from those full retrains, but it has not been measured.

### 3. P3. Per-class birth floor in front of the detector (insurance for the eleven strong classes, no tracker change)
- mechanism: The tracker has ONE birth threshold for sixteen classes: 0.25 has to serve helicopter, whose true detections arrive at 0.82 to 0.85, and small_launcher, whose true detections arrive at 0.18 to 0.47. elias/out/committee/backgrounds/floor_detector.py puts a per-class floor in the detector hook instead, so no tracker code is touched: DRONE_DETECTOR=floor_detector:build ELIAS_INNER=elias.ensemble:build. Mode `drop` removes the detection; mode `clamp` keeps it at 0.24 so it can still refresh a live track but cannot start one. Both modes smoke-tested here against a stub detector.
- evidence: The eleven strong classes have their weakest observed true detection at 0.70 to 0.92 while the birth-grade false boxes sit at median 0.41 (section: true-detection confidences, runs/ctrl_validation). protect.py: a floor removes 7 % of the false births for 5 % of the true birth-capable detections, and all of that 5 % is in the two weak classes, which the table below leaves at 0.25.
- expected gain: 0.00 to +0.01 on a flight like the validation one - the eleven classes it protects were not in real danger, because AP is rank-based. It is worth more only if the unseen flight scores a class we have never seen as a true object (condor, jammer, spacecraft), where a false positive is pure loss: F3 births spacecraft at 0.036 per view and jammer at 0.002. This is insurance, and it is the only proposal here that cannot make a measured class worse.
- cost: 0 to apply, 2 minutes of GPU for one harness run to prove it is neutral | gpu True portal False pod False
- risk: Low but not zero. The floors come from one flight's true detections (n = 15 to 54 per class) and each is at least 0.09 under the lowest true confidence ever observed for its class. The real argument against it is that it is one more moving part in the served config on freeze day.
- how to run: From C:/Users/edlun/Desktop/lucky shots/NordicCupAI-drone/drone-flyby:  PYTHONPATH="$PWD/elias/out/committee/backgrounds" DRONE_DETECTOR=floor_detector:build ELIAS_INNER=elias.ensemble:build ELIAS_WEIGHTS=elias/release/both_m1280.pt,elias/release/F5_fixed_m1280.pt ELIAS_CONF=0.05 ELIAS_ROUTE='<the deployed route>' ELIAS_CLASS_FLOOR='{"hangar":0.6,"helicopter":0.6,"jet_plane":0.6,"large_launcher":0.6,"large_tower":0.6,"mine_roller":0.6,"small_tower":0.6,"medium_plane":0.55,"tank":0.45,"condor":0.6,"jammer":0.6,"spacecraft":0.6,"ta-ta":0.45}' DRONE_CLUSTER_BIRTHS=1 DRONE_BOX_SCALE='{"medium_launcher": 0.85}' bash elias/run_harness.sh elias/release/both_m1280.pt BG_FLOOR --imgsz 1280 --conf 0.05 --birth-confidence 0.25 --update-confidence 0.15 . ### 4. P4. Aspect gate on small_launcher births, harness first
- mechanism: The false small_launcher boxes on empty terrain are square, the true ones on the validation flight are taller than wide. The same wrapper carries ELIAS_CLASS_ASPECT='{"small_launcher": 0.9}', applied to the raw detector box before the tracker normalises the extent; a box wider than the limit is treated exactly like one below its floor.
- evidence: False small_launcher w/h: median 1.00, p10-p90 0.89 to 1.08 (1593 boxes). True small_launcher w/h: median 0.85, p10-p90 0.70 to 0.95 (34 boxes). At w/h <= 0.90 F3 keeps 171 of 1593 false births (89 % removed) and 28 of 34 true ones - 18 % of birth-capable detections lost, which is far less than 18 % of objects, since an object gets a fresh chance in every frame the sweep looks at it. Smoke-tested in floor_detector.py.
- expected gain: -0.01 to +0.02. Honest range: the true side rests on 34 detections from one flight and the separation is a distribution overlap, not a clean split.
- cost: 2 minutes of GPU for the harness run | gpu True portal False pod False
- risk: Medium, overfitting to one flight. Do not serve unless the harness improves. If P1 is applied first the gate would sit on F5's boxes, where the trade is worse (18 of 270 false kept, but only 22 of 29 true kept), so P1 and P4 are alternatives for small_launcher, not a stack.
- how to run: Exactly as P3 with ELIAS_CLASS_ASPECT='{"small_launcher": 0.9}' added to the environment and the tag changed to BG_ASPECT. Compare the harness number and the per-class small_launcher AP against the BG_FLOOR and W_combo_ref runs.
